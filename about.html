About SPLUG by Nifelux

Private, simple phone verification — made in Lagos.

SPLUG is a virtual number service built and operated by Nifelux Technologies Limited, a Nigerian technology company building digital products for a smarter, more connected Africa.

Our purpose

Help people verify the apps they use without handing their personal phone number to every sign-up form.

Local by design

A naira wallet, local payment options and pricing that is shown clearly before every purchase.

Responsible use

SPLUG is for legitimate verification only. We do not allow fraud, spam or abuse of other platforms.

The company behind SPLUG

Nifelux Technologies Limited (RC 9980878) is headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria. Guided by the motto Innovate. Intelligent. Impact., Nifelux develops products across finance, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. SPLUG is one of those products.
